# Perfect-Square-Stacker
A front-end penalty shootout game that lets you pick Left, Center, or Right against an AI keeper.
Opponent details are fetched from TheSportsDB API.

## How to Play
- Click a home player to take control (yellow ring).
- Move with WASD or click/drag on the pitch.
- Control switches to the home ball carrier automatically.
- Dribble into the opponent goal to score.
- Use the Substitute button when a player gets tired.
